# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want a welcoming, practical community. Be respectful, assume good intent,
accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and code. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and deliberate disruption
are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and
other project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the community.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact options on the
GitHub profile rather than in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled as confidentially as practical.
